Formula sheet

Value and cost of capital

V = D + S  ·  Vop = FCF(1 + g) / (WACC − g)
WACC = wd(1 − T)rd + wsrs

Business risk

QBE = F / (P − V)

Financial leverage

ROIC = EBIT(1 − T) / Operating capital  ·  ROE = NI / Equity
Debt raises ROE only if ROIC > rd(1 − T)

Modigliani–Miller

No taxes: VL = VU  ·  rsL = rsU + (D/S)(rsU − rd)
Corporate taxes: VL = VU + TcD  ·  rsL = rsU + (D/S)(rsU − rd)(1 − Tc)
Miller: VL = VU + [1 − (1 − Tc)(1 − Ts) / (1 − Td)] D

Trade-off theory

VL = VU + PV(tax shield) − PV(financial distress costs)

Optimal structure

Hamada: b = bU[1 + (1 − T)(wd/ws)]
CAPM: rs = rRF + b(RPM)

Recapitalization

PPost = (VopNew − DOld) / nPrior
nPost = nPrior − (DNew − DOld) / PPost
SPost = (1 − wd) VopNew
